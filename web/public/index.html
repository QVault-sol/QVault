QVault Devnet

Keep SOL and tokens safe from quantum computers.

Every Solana address is a public key, visible to anyone. A large enough quantum computer could compute the private key behind it. A QVault has no private key on-chain: it opens only with a hash-based one-time signature that your browser computes.

  1. Create a vault. Your browser generates a recovery code. It never leaves this device.
  2. Deposit SOL or any SPL token from your normal wallet.
  3. Send with a Winternitz signature. The rest moves to a fresh vault with a new key.

Connect a wallet to pay network fees

Your wallet only pays the tiny devnet fees and funds deposits. Your savings sit in the vault.

See it work in one click

Creates a vault, deposits 0.05 SOL and 10 test tokens (qUSD, an SPL token), then withdraws 0.01 SOL and 2 qUSD back to your wallet. Each withdrawal is authorised by a Winternitz signature computed here and verified on-chain. Needs about 0.08 devnet SOL. With Phantom you approve each transaction.
